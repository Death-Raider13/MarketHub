# MarketHub Strategic Roadmap: The Digital-First Pivot

To maximize investor appeal, MarketHub is evolving from a general marketplace into a **Specialized Digital Infrastructure Layer** for the African Creator Economy. This roadmap prioritizes focus, high margins, and zero-logistics overhead.

---

## Phase 1: Niche Specialization (Hard Constraint) ✅
**Status: COMPLETED**
- **Hard Digital-Only Constraint:** Fully decommissioned physical product pathways at the database, API, and UI levels.
- **Narrow Entry Niche:** Curated categories for Courses, E-books, Design, and Coaching.
- **Digital Infrastructure:** 90/10 revenue split automated via Paystack.

---

## Phase 2: The "Creator Moat" & DRM ✅
**Status: COMPLETED (CORE)**
- **Digital Rights Management (DRM):** 
    - Token-based secure fulfillment (expiring/single-use).
    - Signed URL delivery for cloud storage.
    - Comprehensive audit logging for all digital assets.
- **Strategic Payments:** Coinbase Commerce integration ready for global creator earnings.

---

## Phase 3: Trust & Discovery Engine 🚧
**Status: ENHANCED (CORE TRUST LAYER READY)**
- **Digital Trust Layer:** Verified Purchase enforcement and granular Accuracy/Delivery ratings implemented.
- **Reputation Engine:** Automated creator scoring (Rising Star, Pro, Elite) based on performance.
- **Next Step:** Expand NIN/BVN-backed ID verification for "Verified Creator" badges.

---

## Phase 4: Operational Scaling & Manual Traction
**Goal:** Prove the model with high-touch early growth followed by automated scaling.

- **The Manual Traction Phase:** Manually onboard the first 50 power-creators to assist with product quality and initial liquidity.
- **Metrics Dashboard:** Real-time internal tracking of GMV, Conversion Rates, and Creator LTV for investor review.
- **Scale Narrative:** Leveraging SSR/ISR for cost-efficient handle of traffic spikes.

---

## Phase 5: Risk & Expansion
**Goal:** Transparency and long-term vision.

- **Risk Mitigation:**
    - Piracy -> DRM/Legal layer.
    - Low Liquidity -> Aggressive niche focus.
    - Quality Control -> Review & Verified Badge system.
- **Expansion Strategy:** Dominate the initial niche (e.g., UI Designers) before opening adjacent categories (e.g., Developers, Educators).

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Investor Pitch:** "MarketHub is the infrastructure layer powering Nigeria's digital creators to sell globally with zero logistics and instant payouts."
