## Revenue Streams Analysis

### 1. Sales Commission (Primary Revenue)
**Rate:** 15% platform commission  
**creator Retention:** 85% of sale price  
**Implementation:** Automatic deduction during payment processing

```typescript
// From payment verification
const commission = 0.15 // 15% platform commission
const creatorEarning = itemTotal * (1 - commission)
```

**Revenue Calculation:**
- Product Sale: ₦10,000
- Platform Commission: ₦1,500 (15%)
- creator Receives: ₦8,500 (85%)

**Ethics Assessment:** ✅ **EXCELLENT**
- Industry standard commission rate (10-20%)
- Transparent to creators
- Competitive with major platforms (Amazon: 15-45%, Jumia: 15-20%)

### 2. Advertising Revenue (Secondary Revenue)
**Rate:** 30% platform commission on ad spend  
**creator Share:** 20-40% for hosting ads on their stores  
**Minimum Rates:** ₦50 CPM, ₦5 CPC

**Revenue Breakdown by Placement:**
- **Homepage Ads:** 100% platform revenue
- **creator Store Ads:** 60% platform, 40% creator
- **Category Ads:** 100% platform revenue  
- **Sponsored Products:** 100% platform revenue

**Current Status:** ⚠️ **UNDERUTILIZED**
- Infrastructure exists but limited active campaigns
- Significant growth opportunity

### 3. Additional Revenue Opportunities (Identified)
**Currently Not Implemented:**
- Listing fees for premium product placement
- Subscription fees for advanced creator tools
- Payment processing fees
- Fulfillment services

## Financial Health Assessment

### Revenue Distribution Model
```
Customer Payment: ₦10,000
├── Platform Commission: ₦1,500 (15%)
├── creator Payment: ₦8,500 (85%)
└── Payment Processing: ~₦150 (1.5% - Paystack fees)

Net Platform Revenue: ~₦1,350 per ₦10,000 sale
```

### Break-Even Analysis
**Platform Costs per Transaction:**
- Payment processing: ~1.5%
- Infrastructure: ~0.5%
- Support & operations: ~1%
- **Total Costs:** ~3% of transaction value

**Profit Margin:** 12% of transaction value (15% - 3%)
